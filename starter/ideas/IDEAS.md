# Ideas

The idea inbox for every project, owned by `ideas/`. Any session adds ideas here (rule W4).

Format: `- [ ] **Idea** — one line of context; fits: <project path(s) or "new project"> (added YYYY-MM-DD)`.
Sections: `## Inbox` (unsorted, newest first), then `## <project path>` for sorted ideas, `## New projects`.
Taken up: tick `[x]` and append `→ <where it went> (YYYY-MM-DD)`; dropped: tick and append `dropped: <why>`.
